# Part III — Making Teams Smarter {.unnumbered}

A squad that works daily starts to accumulate knowledge, and Part III shows how to make that knowledge useful and disciplined. You learn how memory and decisions persist, how to keep context tidy, how skills capture repeatable expertise, how directives, ceremonies, and the reviewer protocol keep quality up, and how to recover when something fails. It follows Part II because you need real work behind you before there is anything worth remembering.

Read it when your team repeats mistakes, loses context between sessions, or needs guardrails.

- **Chapter 15: Memory and Knowledge** Decisions, histories, and what persists.
- **Chapter 16: Tiered Memory, Reflect, and Context Hygiene** Keeping memory small and relevant.
- **Chapter 17: Skills** Packaging reusable know-how.
- **Chapter 18: Directives, Ceremonies, and the Reviewer Protocol** Governance for agent work.
- **Chapter 19: Error Recovery and Iterative Retrieval** Adapting when tasks fail.

**Tasktrack:** by the end of Part III your Tasktrack squad remembers decisions, carries skills, follows directives, and reviews its own work.
