# Part I — Getting Started {.unnumbered}

Part I takes you from zero to a working Squad. You learn what Squad is and why a *team* of AI agents behaves differently from a single assistant, install the CLI, choose the interface you will work in, and run your first session against Tasktrack, the companion project that grows throughout the book. It comes first because everything later assumes a real `.squad/` directory in a real repository.

Everyone should read it, though experienced users may skim Chapter 1. Part I uses a lighter chapter template than the rest of the book.

- **Chapter 1: What Is Squad?** The idea, the vocabulary, and how Squad differs from a single assistant.
- **Chapter 2: Install Squad and Choose Your Interface** Install methods, release channels, and verification.
- **Chapter 3: Your First Session, Annotated** A guided first run, explained step by step.
- **Chapter 4: Meet the Team: Casting, Charters, and Roles** Who is on your team and what each file means.

**Tasktrack:** by the end of Part I you have copied the starter, initialized Squad, run a first session, and understand your cast team.
