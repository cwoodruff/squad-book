# Part IV — Adopting Squad {.unnumbered}

Part IV closes Volume 1 with two playbooks that turn the earlier chapters into recipes for real situations: working solo, starting a new project, joining an existing one, adopting mid-project, contributing to open source, and then scaling to large codebases, monorepos, and teams of humans. It comes last because it draws on everything before it.

Read the scenario that matches your situation, and dip into the other for ideas.

- **Chapter 20: Scenario Playbook A: Solo, New, Existing, Mid-Project, Open Source** Individual and smaller-scale adoption.
- **Chapter 21: Scenario Playbook B: Large Codebases, Monorepos, and Human Teams** Adoption at scale.

**Tasktrack:** you work through variations of the sample project. When you are ready to operate and extend Squad, continue with Volume 2, starting at Chapter 1 (Volume 2).
