# Part I — Advanced Coordination {.unnumbered}

Volume 2 opens by widening Squad beyond a single repository and a single host. Part I covers the Model Context Protocol in depth, where team state lives, how multiple squads coordinate, how teams move between projects, and how Squad runs across clients and programmatic sessions. It comes first in this volume because later operations and internals build on these ideas.

It is for readers who have a working squad from Volume 1 and are pushing it further.

- **Chapter 1: MCP in Depth** Tools and servers for your agents.
- **Chapter 2: State Backends and Storage Providers** Where team state is kept.
- **Chapter 3: Cross-Squad Orchestration** Several squads working together.
- **Chapter 4: Portability, Export/Import, and Inheritance** Moving and reusing teams.
- **Chapter 5: Clients, Hosts, and Programmatic Sessions** Where and how Squad runs in v1.1.0.

**Tasktrack:** by the end of Part I Tasktrack has an MCP server, a configured state backend, a second squad for `web/`, and an exported team.
